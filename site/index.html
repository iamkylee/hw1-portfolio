Research & Projects研究與專案

Ph.D. research direction · Chang Gung University博士研究方向 · 長庚大學

Curiosity-Driven Self-Growing Multi-Agent Systems

好奇心驅動的自成長多代理人系統

Today's LLM multi-agent systems still rely on people to decide how many agents there are and what each one does — the system does not grow with experience. My research asks whether a team of agents can grow its own organisation, evolve its own experts, and decide what to learn next, while staying cost-aware and safe. 現今以大型語言模型為基礎的多代理人系統，代理人的數量與分工仍需人工事先設計，系統不會隨經驗成長。我的研究想探討：一群代理人能否自行成長組織、演化出專家角色，並主動決定下一步要學什麼，同時兼顧成本與安全。

  1. 1

    Organisation growth組織成長

    Agents are born, merged or pruned as tasks change — under an explicit cost budget.隨任務變化，代理人可誕生、合併或剪枝，並在明確的成本限制下進行。

  2. 2

    Role evolution角色演化

    Specialists emerge from experience (generalist ⇄ emerging ⇄ expert) instead of being assigned by hand.專家角色由經驗自然浮現（通才 ⇄ 浮現中專家 ⇄ 專家），而非人工指派。

  3. 3

    Curiosity-driven learning好奇心驅動的主動學習

    Learning progress acts as a curiosity signal that steers exploration — a modulator, not a reward.以「學習進展」作為好奇心訊號來引導探索，是調節訊號，而非獎勵。

  4. 4

    Safety & validation安全與驗證

    Sandboxed exploration, hard safety boundaries and humans in the loop, verified by ablation studies.沙盒探索、硬性安全邊界與人在迴路，並以消融實驗驗證。

APNOMS 2025 · IEEE · Sole author獨立作者

Intent-Driven Autonomous Reconfiguration for Telecom OSS

A Generative-AI-Empowered Hierarchical Multi-Agent Architecture

Problem. Service re-provisioning in telecom touches many legacy OSS systems; each change needs an expert to translate a business request into dozens of system-specific steps. 問題：電信服務改接牽涉多套既有 OSS 系統，每次變更都需要專家把業務需求翻譯成大量系統操作步驟。

My role. I chose the topic, designed the architecture, built the prototype, ran the experiments and wrote the paper — independently. 我的角色：題目、架構、方法、實驗與論文撰寫皆由我獨立完成。

Approach. A Chief Agent decomposes an operator's intent and delegates to specialist agents. I introduced a Primary-Key Pattern and a Command Pattern for agent-to-agent communication so that data passed between agents stays unambiguous. 方法：由 Chief Agent 拆解維運意圖，再委派給各專責代理人；並提出 Primary-Key Pattern 與 Command Pattern 作為代理人間的溝通方式，確保傳遞資料不失真。

Result. 0% error rate on the evaluated reconfiguration tasks; the pattern generalises to other multi-system industries such as finance. 成果：在實驗的改接任務中達到 0% 錯誤率，架構可延伸至金融等多系統產業。
